# Restaurant AI Orchestration Pipeline

A multi-agent AI orchestration system that classifies incoming restaurant signals and routes them to the right AI agent automatically.

## Live Demo

🔗 [remixed-473066b0.vercel.app](https://remixed-473066b0.vercel.app)

## How It Works

Incoming restaurant signals are classified by a router and dispatched to one of three agents:

- **Caption Agent** — generates daily specials content (Relevance AI)
- **Briefing Agent** — produces Monday morning sales recaps and staffing updates (Relevance AI)
- **Review Reply Agent** — drafts responses to Yelp and Google Reviews (Relevance AI)
